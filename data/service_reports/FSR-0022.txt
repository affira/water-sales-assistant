Field Service Report
Reference: FSR-0022

Customer: Gulf Coast Polymers
Site: Corpus Christi, USA
Date of Visit: 20 October 2023
Technician: Yusuf Haddad
Equipment: ClarionRO RO-500 reverse osmosis skid

Summary of Work:
Arrived on site and met the plant team. Permeate tube cracked at interconnector after a pressure surge on restart. I isolated the train, replaced the failed seals and restarted the unit. Ran the system for a while afterwards and confirmed readings were back within the normal range. Briefed the operators before leaving.

Total time on site was about 5.5 hours, plus 5.8 hours of travel.
Total cost for this visit (labour, travel and parts): USD 1,380.00
