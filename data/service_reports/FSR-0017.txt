Field Service Report
Reference: FSR-0017

Customer: Karoo Municipal Water
Site: Beaufort West, South Africa
Date of Visit: 31 August 2023
Technician: Daniel Okafor
Equipment: ClarionRO RO-500 reverse osmosis skid

Summary of Work:
Arrived on site and met the plant team. Elements were past 5 years in service; permeate conductivity trending above spec. I replaced 12 membrane elements and renewed the interconnector O-rings. Ran the system for a while afterwards and confirmed readings were back within the normal range. Briefed the operators before leaving.

Total time on site was about 8.1 hours, plus 7.2 hours of travel.
Total cost for this visit (labour, travel and parts): ZAR 165,800
