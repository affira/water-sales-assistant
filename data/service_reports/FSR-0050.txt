Field Service Report
Reference: FSR-0050

Customer: Karoo Municipal Water
Site: Beaufort West, South Africa
Date of Visit: 8 December 2024
Technician: Rafael Souza
Equipment: HydraBed 1.6 m multimedia filters

Summary of Work:
Arrived on site and met the plant team. Media heavily mud-balled; filtered water turbidity >2 NTU after backwash. I removed and replaced the media in 3 filter vessels. Ran the system for a while afterwards and confirmed readings were back within the normal range. Briefed the operators before leaving.

Total time on site was about 20.2 hours, plus 7.5 hours of travel.
Total cost for this visit (labour, travel and parts): ZAR 108,100
