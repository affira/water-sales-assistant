Field Service Report
Reference: FSR-0027

Customer: Pilbara Mine Water Pty Ltd
Site: Port Hedland, Australia
Date of Visit: 7 January 2024
Technician: Daniel Okafor
Equipment: UltraGuard UG-400 ultrafiltration system

Summary of Work:
Arrived on site and met the plant team. Modules irreversibly fouled after a turbidity event; TMP no longer recovering after CEB. I replaced 10 UF modules and re-ran the integrity test. Ran the system for a while afterwards and confirmed readings were back within the normal range. Briefed the operators before leaving.

Total time on site was about 11.5 hours, plus 10.3 hours of travel.
Total cost for this visit (labour, travel and parts): $31,920.00
