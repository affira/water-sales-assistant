Field Service Report
Reference: FSR-0005

Customer: Agroindustrias Sonora
Site: Hermosillo, Mexico
Date of Visit: 23 March 2023
Technician: Daniel Okafor
Equipment: ClarionRO RO-500 reverse osmosis skid

Summary of Work:
Arrived on site and met the plant team. Autopsy of one lead element showed colloidal fouling consistent with poor pretreatment. I replaced 8 membrane elements and renewed the interconnector O-rings. Ran the system for a while afterwards and confirmed readings were back within the normal range. Briefed the operators before leaving.

Total time on site was about 5.1 hours, plus 5.6 hours of travel.
Total cost for this visit (labour, travel and parts): USD 6,100.00
