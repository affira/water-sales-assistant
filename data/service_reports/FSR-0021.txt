Field Service Report
Reference: FSR-0021

Customer: Kestrel Beverage Co.
Site: Fort Worth, USA
Date of Visit: 12 October 2023
Technician: Ingrid Solberg
Equipment: SoftLine SL-5 duplex softener

Summary of Work:
Arrived on site and met the plant team. Resin capacity exhausted early; hardness breakthrough after 60% of design run length. I replaced the resin in 2 softener vessels and rebuilt the control valve. Ran the system for a while afterwards and confirmed readings were back within the normal range. Briefed the operators before leaving.

Total time on site was about 9.9 hours, plus 2.9 hours of travel.
Total cost for this visit (labour, travel and parts): USD 3,470.00
