Field Service Report
Reference: FSR-0014

Customer: Pilbara Mine Water Pty Ltd
Site: Port Hedland, Australia
Date of Visit: 11 August 2023
Technician: Yusuf Haddad
Equipment: ClarionRO RO-250 reverse osmosis skid

Summary of Work:
Arrived on site and met the plant team. Normalized permeate flow had dropped ~18% and salt passage roughly doubled since the last CIP. I replaced 12 membrane elements and renewed the interconnector O-rings. Ran the system for a while afterwards and confirmed readings were back within the normal range. Briefed the operators before leaving.

Total time on site was about 6.3 hours, plus 10.5 hours of travel.
Total cost for this visit (labour, travel and parts): $15,450.00
