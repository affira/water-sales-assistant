Field Service Report
Reference: FSR-0013

Customer: Desalinas del Norte S.A.
Site: Antofagasta, Chile
Date of Visit: 24 July 2023
Technician: Rafael Souza
Equipment: ClarionRO RO-1000 reverse osmosis skid

Summary of Work:
Arrived on site and met the plant team. Customer reported rising feed pressure over the last 3 weeks. I ran a two-step clean-in-place (acid then alkaline) on 8 pressure vessels. Ran the system for a while afterwards and confirmed readings were back within the normal range. Briefed the operators before leaving.

Total time on site was about 8.0 hours, plus 10.6 hours of travel.
Total cost for this visit (labour, travel and parts): USD 3,500.00
