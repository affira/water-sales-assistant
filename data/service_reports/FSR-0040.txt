Field Service Report
Reference: FSR-0040

Customer: Coromandel Textiles
Site: Tiruppur, India
Date of Visit: 1 August 2024
Technician: Tom Whitfield
Equipment: DosePro DP-50 metering pumps (antiscalant dosing)

Summary of Work:
Arrived on site and met the plant team. Antiscalant pump output down ~15%; diaphragms stiff and cracked at the edge. I rebuilt 2 pumps with new diaphragm kits and re-verified dosing rates. Ran the system for a while afterwards and confirmed readings were back within the normal range. Briefed the operators before leaving.

Total time on site was about 2.3 hours, plus 6.4 hours of travel.
Total cost for this visit (labour, travel and parts): INR 70,600
