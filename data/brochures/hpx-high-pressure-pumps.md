## Page 1

HPX™ high-pressure pumps
Multistage centrifugal pumps for RO feed.

Benefits
• Hydraulic efficiency up to 78%
• Duplex stainless steel wetted parts (super duplex on HPX-S seawater versions)
• Cartridge mechanical seal, replaceable without removing the motor
• Supplied with VFD for constant flux control (AquaTune ready)

Maintenance
Inspect the mechanical seal every 8,000 operating hours. Dry running destroys the
seal within minutes — keep low-level protection on the feed tank active.
Bearing replacement is recommended at 25,000 hours or if vibration exceeds 4.5 mm/s.
