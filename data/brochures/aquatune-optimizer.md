## Page 1

AquaTune™ Optimizer
Adaptive control for reverse osmosis plants

RO plants are usually run on fixed setpoints chosen at commissioning. When the feed
water changes — seasonal temperature swings, rainfall diluting or clouding a source,
a new well coming online — those setpoints stop being right. Operators typically find
out hours or days later from a lab sample or a falling permeate quality reading.

AquaTune continuously normalizes plant data and adjusts the plant to the feed it is
actually receiving.

Benefits
• Stable permeate quality under changing feed conditions
• Lower specific energy (kWh/m3) through flux and pump speed optimization
• Fewer unplanned clean-in-place (CIP) events with predictive cleaning alerts
• Antiscalant consumption matched to actual feed chemistry and recovery
• Full process visibility: normalized flow, salt passage and differential pressure

## Page 2

How it works
Every minute AquaTune reads feed conductivity, temperature, pressures, flows and
(where installed) turbidity and online SDI. It calculates normalized permeate flow,
normalized salt passage and normalized differential pressure, and adjusts:
• Recovery setpoint (within operator-defined limits)
• Antiscalant dose (feed-forward on feed conductivity and recovery)
• HP pump VFD speed for constant flux
• Flush intervals, and CIP timing recommendations

Step changes in feed conductivity or temperature are detected within 15 minutes and
the control model is updated automatically.

Reference installations have reported 5 – 12% lower specific energy and 20 – 35% fewer
unplanned CIPs in the first year. Results depend on the plant and feed water.

## Page 3

Compatibility
• All ClarionRO models: native integration, no extra hardware beyond the edge controller
• Third-party RO plants: supported with the AquaTune I/O retrofit kit; requires online
  feed and permeate conductivity, feed and concentrate pressure, and permeate and
  concentrate flow measurements
• Connectivity: Modbus TCP, OPC UA; Profinet optional

Scope of supply
• AquaTune edge controller and software licence
• I/O retrofit kit (third-party plants only)
• Commissioning on site (typically 2 days) and operator training
• Remote support and periodic software updates

AquaTune does not replace pretreatment. If feed turbidity or SDI exceed the membrane
limits, add or upgrade pretreatment (see UltraGuard UF).
