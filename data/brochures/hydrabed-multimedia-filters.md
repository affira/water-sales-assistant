## Page 1

HydraBed™ multimedia filters
Pressure filters with anthracite, sand and garnet layers for suspended solids removal.

Benefits
• Robust, low-cost pretreatment for well water and low-turbidity surface water
• Filtered water turbidity typically < 1 – 2 NTU
• Air-scour and water backwash, automatic valve nest

Limits
• Recommended feed turbidity: up to 20 NTU continuous
• SDI15 after filtration typically 3 – 5; may not reach SDI < 3 during turbidity events.
  For variable or high-turbidity sources, consider UltraGuard UF instead.
