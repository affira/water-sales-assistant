## Page 1

SoftLine™ duplex water softeners
Ion-exchange softening for boiler feed, cooling and RO pretreatment.

Benefits
• Continuous soft water with duplex (alternating) vessels
• Residual hardness < 1 mg/L as CaCO3
• Volume- or hardness-initiated regeneration

Technical data
Model SL-2 SL-5 SL-10 SL-20
Service flow (m3/h) 2 5 10 20
Resin per vessel (L) 75 200 400 800
Salt consumption: 120 – 160 g NaCl per litre of resin per regeneration
Resin life: typically 5 – 8 years; shorter with chlorine or iron in the feed.

For small RO systems, softening upstream can replace antiscalant dosing for calcium-based scales.
It does not remove silica, so silica-limited RO plants still need antiscalant.
