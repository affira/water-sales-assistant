## Page 1

LumenUV™ disinfection reactors
Chemical-free UV disinfection for process and drinking water.

Applications
• Disinfection of RO permeate and product water
• Biofouling control upstream of RO (reduces viable bacteria; does not remove chlorine or organics)
• Beverage and food process water

Benefits
• Validated dose of 40 mJ/cm2 at end of lamp life
• Long-life amalgam lamps, 12,000 h rated life
• Automatic sleeve wiper and calibrated UV intensity sensor
