## Page 1

DosePro™ DP-series diaphragm metering pumps
Accurate chemical dosing for antiscalant, acid, sodium bisulfite and coagulant.

Benefits
• Steady-state accuracy ±1%
• Turndown 1:100 (DP-2: 1:1000 with stepper drive)
• PTFE-faced diaphragm, PVDF or 316 SS heads
• 4–20 mA, pulse or Modbus RTU control; flow verification sensor optional

Maintenance: replace the diaphragm kit every 8,000 operating hours or yearly,
whichever comes first. A worn diaphragm reduces delivered dose before it fails completely —
underdosing antiscalant on an RO plant can cause scaling of the tail-end elements.
