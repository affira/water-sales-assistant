CarbonGuard™ activated carbon filters
Dechlorination and organics removal ahead of RO.

Why it matters
Polyamide RO membranes are damaged by free chlorine (> 0.1 mg/L). CarbonGuard removes
free chlorine and reduces dissolved organics and taste/odour compounds.

Design data
Vessel diameters: 1.0 – 3.6 m
Empty bed contact time (EBCT): 5 – 10 min for dechlorination; 10 – 20 min for organics reduction
Media: coconut-shell granular activated carbon, 12 x 40 mesh
Media life: depends on chlorine and organic load; typically 12 – 36 months

Note: carbon beds can support bacterial growth. Combine with regular backwashing and,
where biofouling of the RO is a concern, consider sodium bisulfite dechlorination or LumenUV.
