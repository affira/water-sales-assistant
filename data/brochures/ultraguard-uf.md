UltraGuard™ UF
Ultrafiltration pretreatment for reverse osmosis

UltraGuard removes suspended solids, colloids, bacteria and most viruses before RO,
giving a consistent low-SDI feed even when the raw water turbidity swings with the seasons.

Benefits
• Filtrate turbidity < 0.1 NTU and SDI15 typically < 2.5, regardless of feed variation
• Handles feed turbidity up to 100 NTU continuous, peaks to 300 NTU for short periods
• Longer RO membrane life and fewer RO cleanings compared with media filtration
• Automatic backwash, chemically enhanced backwash (CEB) and integrity testing

When to choose UltraGuard
• Surface water, river-influenced wells or seawater intakes with variable turbidity
• Existing media filters cannot hold SDI < 3 during storm events
• RO plants cleaning more often than every 3 months because of particulate or colloidal fouling

UltraGuard can be retrofitted upstream of existing ClarionRO or third-party RO plants.
