ClarionRO™ Series
Brackish water reverse osmosis systems
Edition 2025

Reliable, compact RO skids for industrial process water, boiler feed, beverage and
municipal applications. Factory-tested, pre-wired and delivered ready for connection.

Benefits
• Consistent permeate quality across a wide range of feed conditions

• Integrated clean-in-place (CIP) connections on every model
• Native connectivity with the AquaTune™ Optimizer for adaptive control
• Small footprint: skid-mounted, forklift-liftable up to RO-250

## Page 2

How it works
Pretreated feed water is pressurized by an HPX high-pressure pump and passed through
PureMem™ 8040 spiral-wound membrane elements housed in fibre-reinforced pressure
vessels (6 elements per vessel). Dissolved salts are rejected to the concentrate
stream; the permeate is collected through the central permeate tubes.

The standard element is the PureMem BW-400. Fouling-resistant (FR-400) and
low-energy (LE-440) elements are available as options without changes to the skid.

Pretreatment requirements (feed to RO)
SDI15: < 4 (we recommend < 3 for long membrane life)
Turbidity: < 1 NTU
Free chlorine: < 0.1 mg/L
Feed temperature: 5 – 35 °C
Feed TDS: up to 5,000 mg/L (approx. 7,500 µS/cm)

Feed water from surface sources or wells influenced by rainfall usually needs
ultrafiltration (UltraGuard™) or multimedia filtration (HydraBed™) upstream.

Instrumentation and control
• Feed and permeate conductivity, feed / concentrate / permeate flow
• Pressure transmitters on feed, inter-stage and concentrate
• PLC with 10" touch HMI, Modbus TCP and OPC UA to plant DCS
• Alarm history and trend logging (90 days on HMI)

Service and lifecycle
Membrane replacement, CIP, instrument calibration and HPX pump service are delivered
by Norvale Field Service. Typical membrane life is 3 – 5 years when pretreatment
requirements are met. Contact your Norvale representative for service agreements.
