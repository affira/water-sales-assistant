## Page 1

PureMem™ 8040 RO membrane elements
Spiral-wound polyamide thin-film composite elements, 8 inch x 40 inch.

Element range
BW-400 — standard brackish water element
LE-440 — low-energy element for low-salinity feed, lower feed pressure
FR-400 — fouling-resistant element with low-fouling surface and 34 mil feed spacer,
for feeds with organics, biological activity or variable quality
SW-380 — seawater element (not used in ClarionRO brackish skids)

## Page 3

Operating limits
Maximum operating pressure: 41 bar (BW, LE, FR); 83 bar (SW)
Maximum feed temperature: 45 °C
pH range, continuous operation: 2 – 11; cleaning: 1 – 13
Maximum feed SDI15: 5
Maximum feed turbidity: 1 NTU
Free chlorine: < 0.1 mg/L
Maximum feed flow per element: 17 m3/h
Maximum pressure drop: 1.0 bar per element, 3.5 bar per pressure vessel

When to clean (based on normalized data)
Clean when, compared with the baseline after commissioning or the last cleaning:
• normalized permeate flow drops 10 – 15%, or
• normalized differential pressure rises 15%, or
• normalized salt passage rises 5 – 10%.
Delaying cleaning beyond these limits makes fouling harder to remove and shortens element life.

Expected element life: 3 – 5 years under recommended operating conditions.
